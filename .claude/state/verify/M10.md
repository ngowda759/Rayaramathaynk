### New finding

**M10-F42 (HIGH) - unauthenticated, service-role writes on the public temple-areas API.**

`app/api/temple-areas/route.ts` exports a `POST` that calls `templeAreasService.addArea(body)` (`:26`).
`app/api/temple-areas/[id]/route.ts` exports a `PUT` calling `updateArea` (`:31`) and a `DELETE`
calling `deleteArea` (`:45`). None of the three reads a session, a cookie, a bearer token or a role,
and the repository has **no `middleware.ts` at the root** to gate them (`find . -maxdepth 2 -name
middleware.ts` returns nothing). All three service methods build their client with
`createAdminClient()` (`services/temple-areas.supabase.service.ts:98,:129,:159`), which uses
`SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (`lib/supabase/admin.ts:10`) and therefore bypasses RLS entirely.

The only gate is shape validation in the route: a non-empty `name` and a present `category`
(`app/api/temple-areas/route.ts:19-24`); `PUT` and `DELETE` validate nothing at all.

Net: any anonymous caller can create, overwrite or delete every row of `temple_areas`. The builder
saw the service-role clients (M10-F31) but attributed the risk to "any auth gap in those routes" and
prescribed a fix that was already implemented, so the actual open door went unrecorded.
