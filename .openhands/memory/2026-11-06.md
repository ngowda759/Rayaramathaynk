## Durable facts worth remembering

- **A `REVOKE` a role may not perform only WARNS** (`no privileges could be revoked`,
  SQLSTATE `01007`) and leaves the routine world-executable — it does NOT raise. So a
  migration that trusts the REVOKE "succeeding" can silently no-op. Correct gate:
  `pg_has_role(current_user, proowner, 'MEMBER')` (a role is a member of itself, so owner
  passes). Verify post-condition with `has_function_privilege(owner::regrole, sig, …)`.
- `supabase/postgres:15.19.0.018` disposable container is the practical local harness:
  `postgres` non-superuser, `supabase_admin` superuser, `postgres` NOT a member of

  cluster init, so a later `CREATE DATABASE` does not inherit them (harness must reproduce
